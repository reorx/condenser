# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2026-08-15*

Condenser is a self-hosted, single-user feed reader. The Condenser iOS app is a
client for a server that you run yourself.

## What the app collects

Nothing.

- The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
- The app does not use third-party services or SDKs.
- The developer does not receive any data from the app.

## Where your data lives

All content and all reading state (subscriptions, read/saved items, settings)
live on the server that you configure in the app. The app communicates only
with that server, over the address you enter.

Images and link previews are also fetched through your own server, so the app
does not contact the source platforms directly.

## Credentials

To sign in, your server issues the app a device token. The app stores this
token in the iOS Keychain on your device. You can revoke it at any time from
your server's settings page. The app never sees or stores your server's
password.
